Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Purpose

This is a personal, single-user file backup setup using the rclone command-line application.

Google Drive Access

Rclone runs locally on my personal computer and accesses my Google Drive account through the Google Drive API.

It accesses files and metadata only when I explicitly execute backup, upload, download, listing or synchronization commands.

Data Storage and Sharing

Files selected for upload are transferred to my own Google Drive account. Files downloaded are stored on my own computer.

The website itself does not collect, process or store Google Drive files, account credentials, or OAuth tokens. No data is sold or shared with other parties by this personal setup.

Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are maintained locally by rclone. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

This application is maintained exclusively for personal use by its owner.
